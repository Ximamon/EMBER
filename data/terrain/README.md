# Prepared Collserola crop

`collserola.asc` + `collserola.prj` are the offline 512 × 512, 10 m EPSG:32631
input. `collserola.json` records source, extent, classes, checksum and transformations.
The small derived crop is versioned; full national rasters must be kept in `data/raw/`.

Data license: **CC BY 4.0**, © Paula Sánchez 2025.
Source: Sánchez et al., ZAFM Europe v1.0, https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.18788338.
The crop/reprojection is a modification of that dataset. Original codes are retained.
